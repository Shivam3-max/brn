Website blueprint · custom Shopify theme · 15 Oct 2026

A store that shows what's inside before it asks for the sale.

BRN launches with eight products into a shelf where HealthKart, Kapiva and OZiva already shout. This website wins by explaining. Every product is opened up on screen. Its ingredients are drawn in, one by one, each tied to the deficiency it fills and the person who needs it. The visitor watches a short story play out, then picks their concern and buys.

"Bare" means we show the label. "Root" means Ayurveda and the plant. The website should feel like both: honest numbers, living drawings.

02 · The big idea

The website is a film, and every section is a scene.

You asked for a site where the visitor doesn't have to work. The story should play on its own as they move down the page. So every major section runs on a timeline. It starts by itself when it comes into view, can be scrubbed by scrolling, and can always be paused. The visitor watches the products, the ingredients and the deficiencies they fill appear in turn, like a short animated film told in text and drawings.

1. Concern first, bottle last

Every scene opens with a feeling the visitor has today ("cramps at 2 am", "tired by 3 pm"). The product appears only once they recognise themselves.

2. Show the inside

Every product is opened on screen. Each ingredient is drawn in with its dose, what it does, and the gap it fills.

3. Video everywhere it helps

Hero loops, concern intros, shoppable reels and short explainers on every product page. Each video is tied to a concern, so it's never filler.

4. Admin-fed, placeholder-ready

Banners, products, ingredients, videos and stacks all come from the Shopify admin. Until you upload, each slot shows a labelled placeholder with its exact size.

5. Fast, despite the motion

The 3D loads only after the page is usable, and only on devices that can handle it. Phones get lighter drawn versions of the same scenes.

6. Two taps to anything

Concern menu, body map, quiz and search are all on the first screen. Any product is two taps from anywhere.

The illustration system

You asked for 3D and drawn illustrations all over the site. I'll use two layers that work together.

Layer 1 · Living line drawings

Every ingredient, concern and deficiency sign is drawn as a single-weight ink line, in the product's colour. As it scrolls into view it draws itself in, stroke by stroke, like a GIF. These are drawn in code, so they're sharp at any size, weigh almost nothing and load instantly on phones.

Layer 2 · 3D objects (WebGL)

The bottles, capsules, tablets and powder are real 3D objects that turn, open and scatter their contents. The homepage root system grows in 3D. On phones and slow devices, layer 1 stands in automatically.

05 · The signature moment

The Story Player: a product opens, and its ingredients tell you why.

This is the "movie" you described. When this section reaches the screen it starts playing on its own. The bottle sits in the middle. One by one, each ingredient comes out and is drawn in, labelled with its dose, what it does, and the signs you may be low in it. Then the next product takes the stage. Visitors can tap a product to jump to it, pause, or scroll to scrub.

This working demo shows the timing and layout. On the live site the bottle is a turning 3D object, the ingredients draw themselves in, and doses come from each product's admin data.

Ingredient names follow typical formulas for each product type. Doses show as "per label" until Nimai's final formulas arrive. Nothing here is a final claim.

13 · How it's built

A custom Shopify theme, built from scratch on the Helbrede engine.

Theme

  • Shopify Online Store 2.0, written from scratch (not a Dawn copy)
  • Every homepage scene is a section you can reorder, hide or edit in the theme editor
  • GSAP + ScrollTrigger for the timelines, Three.js for 3D, Lenis for smooth scroll
  • Built and tested locally first with the same preview setup as Helbrede (port 3800), then pushed to your store

Your admin (how you'll add things later)

  • Ingredient entries: name, drawing, dose, what it does, signs of low levels
  • Concern entries: colour, icon, body zone, intro video, signs list
  • Video entries: file, poster, tagged products, type
  • Stack entries: products and discount
  • Per product: colour world, ingredients with doses, who it's for, how to take, timeline, label, batch reports

Speed targets

Page usable in under 2.5 s on a mid-range phone on 4G. 3D loads after the page is ready, never blocking the banner.

Graceful fallback

No WebGL, a slow device, or "reduce motion" switched on: the same scenes play as light drawings, or as still frames.

Apps (later)

Reviews (Judge.me), Shopify Subscriptions, a COD-friendly checkout and WhatsApp. I'll shortlist them with costs before launch.

14 · Rules the site follows

Bold storytelling, safe claims.

We say

  • "Supports liver health", "helps with everyday stress"
  • "Contains milk thistle, studied for liver support"
  • "Signs you may be low in…", always paired with "check with your doctor"
  • Real doses, real batch reports, real reviews

We never say

  • "Cures", "treats", "reverses" or "guaranteed results"
  • "Detoxes your body" as a medical claim
  • "India's #1" or "doctor-approved" without proof
  • Invented ratings or reviews. Stars show only once real reviews exist

Every product page and the footer carry the disclaimer: "This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease." Nimai's QA team signs off each product's claim list before it goes live.
